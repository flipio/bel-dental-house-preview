Digital dentistry

What digital smile planning looks like

Dr Dragoslav Vuković · 4 min read

Every prosthetic work in our practice begins with an intraoral scan — no impression material, a few minutes. The digital model of the jaw is the base on which everything else is planned.

From facial photographs and the scan we build a digital design: tooth shape, length and position matched to the lip line, the smile and facial proportions. This is not a “pretty picture” — it is the plan the technician uses to make the trial work.

The trial smile (mock-up) goes in the mouth before any preparation. Only once we agree on the result in the mirror does the work go into production. That is why outcomes are predictable and late corrections rare.
