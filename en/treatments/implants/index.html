About the treatment

What is a dental implant?

The doctor: Dr Dragoslav Vuković

Implantology offers solutions for every form of tooth loss — from a single missing tooth to a fully edentulous jaw — and is an option for patients who are not satisfied with a conventional denture.

Using an implant preserves the neighbouring teeth, which would have to be ground down for a conventional bridge. Implants help preserve the natural teeth and prevent the bone loss that follows tooth extraction.

Bridges and dentures on implants

  • Fixed bridge — after analysing the condition of the bone, six implants are placed and a bridge is fixed onto them.
  • All-on-4 — a fixed full-arch bridge on four implants. Suitable when there is not enough bone in the posterior regions for conventional placement.
  • Hybrid denture — where bone is severely reduced, four implants carry a superstructure that stabilises the denture.
  • Overdenture — two implants in the front of the lower jaw with attachments that stabilise the denture and restore its function.

Computer-guided implantology

Using 3D software we determine the optimal implant position in the jawbone virtually. Based on the result, a surgical guide is produced that allows more precise placement without the conventional incision of the mucosa.

Treatment without fear

For more extensive procedures, controlled conscious sedation supervised by a specialist anaesthetist is available. Sedation is not general anaesthesia: the patient stays conscious and communicates, but without fear or discomfort. This makes it possible to complete more complex work in a single visit.

I.

The implant

A titanium screw placed into the jawbone under local anaesthesia. Once it has fused with the bone it takes over the role of the tooth root.

II.

The abutment

Made of titanium or zirconia. It connects the implant to the prosthetic work — a crown or a bridge.

III.

The tooth

A crown or bridge in ceramic, zirconia or another aesthetic material, permanently fixed onto the abutment.

The process

  1. 01

    Consultation and 3D diagnostics

    Clinical examination, scans and assessment of the quantity and quality of bone.

  2. 02

    Digital planning

    Implant positions are determined in 3D software; a surgical guide is made where needed.

  3. 03

    Implant placement

    Performed under local anaesthesia. With a guide it is done without the conventional incision of the mucosa.

  4. 04

    Osseointegration

    The healing period during which the implant fuses with the jawbone.

  5. 05

    Prosthetic work

    The crown, bridge or denture is made and fitted onto the implants.

Frequently asked questions

Is implant placement painful?
The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia and is painless. For more extensive procedures, controlled conscious sedation supervised by an anaesthetist is available.
Can I have fixed teeth straight away?
In some cases immediate loading is used — the crown is made within 48 hours of placement. This technique is not suitable for every patient or every situation.
What if I do not have enough bone?
Where bone is lacking, bone regeneration precedes treatment. For an edentulous jaw the All-on-4 concept is often possible without additional grafting.
