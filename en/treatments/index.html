From diagnostics to complex rehabilitations — we manage the whole course of treatment at the practice.

All-on-4

Complete fixed rehabilitation of the jaw on four implants. A fixed provisional bridge within 1 to 3 days — no years of waiting, no denture.

Implants

A solution for one missing tooth, several or all of them — from a single implant to a fixed bridge for the whole jaw.

From €700

Smile design

The shape, size and colour of your future teeth are planned before any work begins — you see the smile on screen first.

From €300

Prosthetics

Replacing lost teeth and improving the function and appearance of natural ones — veneers, crowns, bridges and removable dentures.

From €250

Periodontology

Preserving the health of the supporting tissue around the teeth — from initial therapy and scaling to periodontal surgery.

Endodontics

Root canal treatment aimed at preserving the tooth and its root. The procedure is painless, under local anaesthesia.

Orthodontics

Correcting the position of teeth and the bite in children and adults — fixed braces, removable appliances and Invisalign aligners.

Bone regeneration

A surgical method that increases bone structure and volume, creating the conditions for implants to be placed later.

Diagnostics

Panoramic and intraoral digital images that precede every procedure and make precise treatment planning possible.

Restorative dentistry

Treating caries and restoring teeth with aesthetic fillings, including ceramic inlays and onlays made with CAD/CAM technology.

Teeth whitening

A painless whitening procedure, preceded by the removal of calculus and stains from nicotine, coffee, tea and red wine.

From €150

Laser therapy

The Elexxion soft-tissue laser and HELBO antimicrobial photodynamic therapy in the treatment of periodontal disease.
