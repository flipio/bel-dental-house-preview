About the treatment

Correcting tooth position and the bite

Orthodontics corrects the irregular position of teeth and the relationship between the jaws. Although orthodontic appliances were long thought to be mainly for the young, treatment today gives good results at any age.

In childhood

  • Before the age of six — trainers, that is myofunctional Myobrace appliances, to correct habits: moving from mouth breathing to nose breathing, tongue position, thumb sucking, incorrect swallowing. They are made of soft silicone, with no wires or rigid parts, and are worn overnight and for an hour during the day.
  • After the age of six — the position of the teeth is corrected and the jaws widened with Myobrace or active plate appliances. Plate appliances are removable, which makes them practical for daily use; they are worn 14 to 16 hours a day.
  • Around the age of ten — a year before the pubertal growth spurt, the bite can be corrected with functional appliances, in patients whose jaw growth needs to be stimulated in a particular direction.

In adolescence and adulthood

After the age of twelve, once all the permanent teeth are through, the position of the teeth and jaws is corrected with fixed braces or Invisalign aligners.

  • Metal brackets — conventional ones use coloured elastic ligatures, while self-ligating brackets need none and require fewer check-ups.
  • Aesthetic ceramic brackets — less visible, though the material makes them more brittle than metal ones.
  • Iconix brackets — made of metal with a gold coating, as durable as conventional metal brackets but in a more discreet champagne shade.
  • Invisalign aligners — completely clear, removable, with a shorter wearing time and easier oral hygiene. A set of aligners is made individually; each successive aligner moves the teeth gradually into the desired position. They are ideal for correcting smaller irregularities of the front teeth.

Widening the upper jaw

If the upper jaw is narrower than the lower one, it has to be widened before fixed braces are placed. In children this is done with active plate appliances with an expansion screw; in older patients with RPE, that is Hyrax, appliances, which stay in place for six months after the effect is achieved. After the age of twenty, once growth is complete, widening is only possible with skeletal anchorage, so it is worth addressing in good time.

Retention

Once the appliance is removed, the retention phase follows, and it matters as much as the treatment itself. Retainers can be fixed, placed on the inner side of the dental arch, or removable in the form of retention aligners. At first they are worn constantly, then the wearing time is gradually reduced. Children are advised to wear retainers until growth is complete.

I.

Removable appliances

Myobrace trainers and active plate appliances for children. They are taken out of the mouth, which makes hygiene easier; they are worn overnight or 14 to 16 hours a day.

II.

Fixed braces

Brackets and an archwire fixed to the teeth. Metal and self-ligating, aesthetic ceramic, and gold-coloured Iconix brackets are available.

III.

Invisalign aligners

Clear aligners made for your teeth. They are changed every two weeks, worn up to 20 hours a day and removed for eating and brushing.

The process

  1. 01

    First examination

    History and clinical examination, impressions, a panoramic and a lateral cephalometric X-ray, and intraoral and extraoral photographs.

  2. 02

    Analysis and plan

    Based on the scans and study models, the appliance that suits the irregularity and the age of the patient is chosen.

  3. 03

    Active treatment

    Wearing a removable or fixed appliance, or Invisalign aligners, with regular check-ups.

  4. 04

    Retention

    After the appliance is removed, retainers are worn — fixed on the inner side of the arch or removable — so that the result stays stable.

Frequently asked questions

When should a child have a first orthodontic check-up?
The recommended age is early mixed dentition, around seven years old.
Is treatment possible as an adult?
Yes. After the age of twelve, once all the permanent teeth are through, tooth and jaw position is corrected with fixed braces or Invisalign aligners at any age.
How often are check-ups needed?
Invisalign aligners and self-ligating brackets require fewer check-ups, which suits patients who live abroad.
What are retainers and why are they needed?
Teeth tend to drift back towards their original position. Retainers, fixed or removable, hold the result after the appliance is removed.
