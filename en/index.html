II.

Implantology

From a single implant to full-jaw rehabilitation with the All-on-4 and All-on-6 concepts. Fixed teeth — including for patients who have worn dentures for years.

Aesthetic dentistry

Smile design guided by digital planning: veneers, crowns and whitening matched to your face, smile and the natural look of teeth.

Frequently asked questions

How many visits does All-on-4 take?
You receive a fixed provisional bridge within 1 to 3 days of implant placement. The permanent bridge is made after 3 to 6 months, in a second, shorter visit.
Can I get a plan before coming to Belgrade?
Yes. Based on the photos and scans you send us, we prepare a preliminary treatment plan and quote — before you book a ticket.
Which languages do you speak?
We work with patients in Serbian, English, German and Italian.
What does the first examination look like?
A clinical examination, intraoral scanning and digital diagnostics, followed by a discussion of your options. You leave with a clear treatment plan and quote.
How do I book an appointment?
By phone, by WhatsApp or Viber message, or through the form on the Contact page. We reply within 24 hours on working days.
